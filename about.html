About Sheetal Yadav & Co.

Sheetal Yadav & Co. is a multidisciplinary professional firm committed to delivering reliable, ethical, and result-oriented financial, taxation, compliance, and legal solutions to individuals, startups, businesses, and organizations.

Our firm brings together the expertise of CA Sheetal Yadav, a Chartered Accountant, and Advocate Anjali Yadav, enabling clients to receive comprehensive professional support under one roof. By integrating financial and legal advisory services, we help clients make informed decisions while ensuring full compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

We believe that every client deserves practical advice, transparent communication, and personalized attention. Whether you need assistance with income tax, GST, company compliance, business registration, accounting, audits, or legal representation in civil, criminal, family, or commercial matters, our team is dedicated to provide timely and effective solutions.

About Sheetal Yadav & Co.

Sheetal Yadav & Co. is a multidisciplinary professional firm committed to delivering reliable, ethical, and result-oriented financial, taxation, compliance, and legal solutions to individuals, startups, businesses, and organizations.

Our firm brings together the expertise of CA Sheetal Yadav, and Advocate Anjali Yadav, enabling clients to receive comprehensive professional support under one roof. By integrating financial and legal advisory services, we help clients make informed decisions while ensuring full compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
